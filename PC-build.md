Final Verification Checks
Clearance & Airflow: With the Lian Li case in high-mount air cooling mode, your primary and secondary slots will maintain breathing room. Keep an eye on adding those bottom intake fans once the second accelerator joins the chassis to manage the thermal sandwich effect.
M.2 Thermal Armor: Because the Fikwot drive utilizes a flat graphene sticker rather than a bulky third-party heatsink, it will integrate seamlessly under the motherboard's native thermal shields.
